Zara

Floor: 2

Zara is a fashion brand from Spain known for its fast fashion concept, which allows it to quickly follow the latest trends in the fashion world and bring them directly to the market. The brand was founded by Amancio Ortega and has grown into one of the largest fashion retailers in the world.

Chattime

Floor: 1

Chatime is a Taiwanese beverage brand renowned for its various bubble tea variants and toppings such as boba, jelly, and pudding. Chatime also allows customers to customize the sweetness level, ice amount, and toppings according to their preferences.

FootLocker

Floor: 2

Foot Locker is an American retail company that focuses on selling sports shoes, particularly sneakers, as well as various athletic apparel such as clothing and accessories. This brand is widely known as one of the main destinations for sneaker enthusiasts because it offers a wide range of products from renowned brands like Nike, Adidas, and Puma.

HnM

Floor: 3

H&M (Hennes & Mauritz) is a fashion brand from Sweden known for its fast fashion concept, offering the latest clothing designs that follow global trends at relatively affordable prices. The brand was founded by Erling Persson and has grown into one of the largest fashion retailers in the world.

Uniqlo

Floor: 2

Uniqlo is a Japanese fashion brand known for its simple, functional, and high-quality clothing for everyday needs. The brand is part of the Fast Retailing company and was founded by Tadashi Yanai.

Sephora

Floor: 1

Sephora is a French beauty retailer known as one of the main destinations for makeup, skincare, perfume, and body care products from various renowned brands worldwide. The company is under the LVMH group and has numerous branches across different countries, including Indonesia.

Nike

Floor: 1

Nike is an American sportswear brand known as one of the largest companies in the world in the field of shoes, clothing, and sports equipment. The company was founded by Phil Knight and Bill Bowerman, and is known for its “Swoosh” logo and iconic slogan “Just Do It”.

iBox

Floor: 3

iBox is one of the official retail networks in Indonesia that focuses on selling products from Apple Inc., such as iPhone, MacBook, iPad, Apple Watch, and various supporting accessories. iBox is known as an authorized reseller, ensuring that the products sold are genuine and come with official warranty.

Guardian

Floor: 3

Guardian is a health and beauty retail network that provides various products such as skincare, cosmetics, vitamins, and daily health needs. This brand is part of the DFI Retail Group and has numerous branches across different countries, including Indonesia.
