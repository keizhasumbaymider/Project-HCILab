About FontDale

Welcome to FontDale, a premium lifestyle mall designed to offer a modern shopping and leisure experience. Officially opened in 2025, FontDale brings together global fashion brands, lifestyle stores, and a wide variety of dining options under one roof, providing visitors with an unforgettable urban experience.

FontDale is a premium lifestyle mall envisioned by Franz Diego, first conceptualized in 2023 and officially opened in 2025. Located at Arcadia Circle, FontDale brings together global fashion brands, lifestyle stores, and diverse dining experiences within its two main wings — the North Gallery and South Pavilion — connected by the signature Lumen Skywalk. Built with a focus on contemporary design and high-quality experiences, FontDale has quickly become a standout destination for modern urban shoppers.

FontDale was envisioned by Franz Diego in 2023 and built with a focus on contemporary architecture and high-quality experiences. The mall features two main wings – the North Gallery and South Pavilion – connected by the iconic Lumen Skywalk, a signature design element that has quickly become a landmark of the mall.

What sets FontDale apart is our commitment to providing both luxury and accessibility. We curate the finest international brands while offering a welcoming environment for families, professionals, and young trendsetters. Every corner of the mall is designed to ensure comfort, style, and convenience for our visitors.

At FontDale, we are not just about shopping. We host seasonal events, cultural activities, and entertainment programs to engage our community and make each visit unique. Our mission is to be the premier destination for modern urban lifestyles, combining shopping, dining, and entertainment into one seamless experience.
